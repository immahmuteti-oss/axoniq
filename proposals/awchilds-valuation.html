How it works

Three steps, and the first one is done

No call centre, no chasing, and no valuation that arrives a fortnight later when you have already instructed somebody else.

You send the form

Address, bedrooms, and whether you are selling or letting. That is enough for us to start — everything else we can ask on the phone.

We reply the same working day

With a realistic figure based on what has actually sold and let on your street, not an automated estimate pulled from a national average.

We visit, if you want us to

Twenty minutes, at a time that suits you. You are under no obligation to instruct us, and we will say so on the doorstep.
