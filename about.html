How I work

Three things worth knowing

I'd rather tell you nothing is wrong than sell you something that isn't.

I diagnose before I quote. Every engagement starts with your real site on a real phone. You see the evidence first — and if the finding turns out to be wrong when we check it together, it gets withdrawn. A claim you can disprove in ten seconds is worse than no claim.

Speed is part of the product. Small fixes land the same day; a conversion page is live in 48 hours. Not because fast is impressive, but because a problem that's costing you money every day should not sit in a queue for three weeks.

Fixed scope, fixed price, fixed date. Agreed in writing before anything starts. If the budget doesn't reach, I cut the scope and tell you plainly what you're not getting — I don't quietly do less for the same money.
