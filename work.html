4 pages shipped CRM wired Google + Meta Schema
Open Golfscape, live →

Four property landing pages — Golfscape, Pandora, Aria Park and Executive Suites — each built as a conversion page rather than a brochure: hero, unit types, gallery, location map, FAQ, and a capture path on every screen.

The part that matters is behind the form. Every enquiry is delivered three ways — into the CRM as a lead, into the site's own record, and to the team directly — so a submission cannot vanish because one service was having a bad day. Bot protection sits in front of it, because a form that fills with spam gets ignored, and an ignored form is a broken form.

On top of that: Google and Meta campaigns built, launched and tracked against actual enquiries rather than clicks, conversion tagging so each advert can be traced to what it produced, and structured data applied across the listing catalogue so individual properties are legible to search engines.

How I audit

Scope Conversion-leak scanning
Use Every engagement starts here

Own tooling Evidence first

I built my own scanner for this. It checks a site for the things that quietly cost money: redirects sending your visitors somewhere else entirely, server errors, missing mobile handling, absent capture, dead contact paths, no analytics, broken link previews.

It is deliberately careful about what it claims. When it cannot measure something honestly it reports nothing rather than guessing — and anything it flags, I verify by hand on a real phone before I ever put it in front of you. A finding you can disprove in ten seconds is worse than no finding at all.

If you want, I'll run it on your site and send you the output. That costs you nothing and takes me a few minutes.
