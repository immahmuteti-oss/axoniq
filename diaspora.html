The pattern

Three failures, on nearly every site

This month I audited the websites of Kenyan land and property companies that are actively marketing to the diaspora. Not a survey — I opened each one and tested it. The same three things were wrong almost everywhere, and none of them are design problems.

Defect one

No instant channel at peak intent

No WhatsApp button, or a share widget mistaken for one. The buyer is reading at 2am Kenyan time, so "call our office" and "visit us at 9am" are worth nothing to them.

Defect two

No substitute for the site visit

No working virtual tour — in one case a gallery reading "being updated". For a buyer who cannot fly home, the gallery is the entire product. It is the most expensive page to leave unfinished.

Defect three

No capture fallback

If the visitor doesn't tap WhatsApp, nothing is recorded. No form, no list, no second chance. The lead is gone and you never knew it existed.

You are paying to bring them in, then losing them at the last step.
